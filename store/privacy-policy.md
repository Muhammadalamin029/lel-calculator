# Privacy Policy — LEL Calculator

Effective date: 2026-09-26
Applies to: LEL Calculator, Android application ID `com.lelstore.calculator`,
published by LEL Store.

## Summary

LEL Calculator does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal
information. All calculations are performed locally on your device.

## Details

- **No account.** The app does not require sign-in or account creation.
- **No personal information collected.** The app does not ask for or access
  your name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.
- **Calculations stay on your device.** Entered numbers and results are kept
  only in the app's temporary on-screen state and are never transmitted to a
  server or any third party.
- **No third-party tracking.** The app contains no advertising SDKs, analytics
  SDKs, or cross-app tracking.
- **No unnecessary permissions.** The app requests zero Android permissions
  and works fully offline.
- **Children.** The app is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from
  anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same
public URL linked from the Google Play listing.

LEL Store — contact details as listed on the Google Play developer page.
