## Full description (4000 chars max)

LEL Calculator by LEL Store is a simple calculator for everyday arithmetic.

Add, subtract, multiply and divide, work with decimals and percentages, and
keep going from your last result. Everything happens instantly on your device —
no account, no sign-in, no internet connection required.

What you can do:

- Addition, subtraction, multiplication and division
- Decimal calculations (for example 5.5 + 2.25 = 7.75)
- Percentage calculations
- Positive/negative toggle
- Clear and delete
- Repeat the last calculation by pressing equals again
- Continue calculating from a previous result

Designed for everyday use:

- Clean, simple interface that opens straight into the calculator
- Large, comfortable buttons for small phones
- Light mode and dark mode following your system appearance
- Accessible labels and good contrast, works with larger font sizes
- No ads, no tracking, no unnecessary permissions
- Works fully offline — calculations never leave your device

LEL Calculator does one thing and does it well: everyday arithmetic.
